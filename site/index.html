A narrow, durable goal

A handful of carefully engineered, inter-operable vocabularies, each published at a permanent address so it can be cited, imported and dereferenced for decades. Vocabularies here favour OWL 2 DL where reasoning matters, SKOS where a controlled vocabulary is enough, and SHACL for closed-world validation.

Two commitments shape everything published under this namespace:

IRIs are permanent from birth. No example.org placeholder that has to be migrated later, taking every downstream adopter with it. The cost of that lesson is visible on this very site — the Record Harm Ontology is still served through a rewrite because it was drafted under a placeholder, while the Record Ontology, born at its final address, is byte-identical to its source.

Documentation and RDF live at the same address. Every namespace IRI content-negotiates: a browser gets a page, a reasoner gets Turtle, and the fragment identifiers resolve client-side out of one document.
